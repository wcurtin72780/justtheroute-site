Made by a runner, for runners

A place to
study the miles.

I’m a runner, therapist, husband, and dad from Philadelphia. I’ve always wanted to create an archive of running routes and local road races—a place where runners can study a route or race course in real time, or simply enjoy the sights and sounds.

I’m starting close to home, with the hope of exploring beyond Philadelphia as this collection grows.

The main runs unfold in real time with natural sound and minimal editing. Coverage and any significant recording interruptions are noted on each video’s page.

Over time, this archive will grow to include route reflections, practical details, and maps—notes from actually being out there.
