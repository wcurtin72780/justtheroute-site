Along the route

The full Philadelphia Marathon, filmed start to finish on a beautiful Sunday in November — the weather held, and the whole race is here: the strong miles and the ones where everything fell apart.

The pace began to slow around mile 14 as muscle cramps set in. By mile 15 I was taking walking and stretching breaks, and by miles 18–19 the wall had arrived and quitting felt tempting. What carried it was the company — countless runners out there with the same grit, going through the same suffering, and strangers cheering us all on. I finished in 4:27:11, uninjured, and ran the last mile in.

One brief camera glitch mid-race was patched in place, with the video and GPS overlay kept in step — what you see is the race as it ran.
