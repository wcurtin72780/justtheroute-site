Support the trails

These runs are free to watch because Philadelphia's trails are free to run. If you enjoy them, consider a donation to the Friends of the Wissahickon — they maintain the Wissahickon Valley Park trails you see in this video.

Not affiliated with Friends of the Wissahickon — I just run these trails and want to support the people who keep them beautiful.
